Rigoutech Inc. · Montréal

A technology studio in Montréal.

We build, automate and secure systems on Azure and with AI. We train teams. We build our own products.

This page is a short English summary. The rest of the site is in French. Rigoutech was founded in 2021 in Montréal by Cedric Kouma, software developer, Azure and DevSecOps specialist. Microsoft certifications: AZ-204 and AZ-305. AZ-400 is in preparation.

Training day

“IA générative et cybersécurité au travail.” Seven hours, up to 12 people. Includes modules on Quebec’s privacy law (Loi 25) and AI-assisted phishing. An optional Microsoft Copilot module.

For a Quebec employer: dépense admissible si inscrite au plan de formation.
