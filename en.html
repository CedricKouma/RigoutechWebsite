Training day

“IA générative et cybersécurité au travail.” Seven hours, up to 12 people. Includes modules on Quebec’s privacy law (Loi 25) and AI-assisted phishing. An optional Microsoft Copilot module.

For a Quebec employer whose payroll exceeds $2 million, the Act to promote workforce skills development and recognition (often called the 1% Act) requires an investment of at least 1% of that payroll in eligible training expenses. A course written into the company’s training plan can count. It is not an automatic credit. Revenu Québec explains the contribution.
